# Design Partner Hunt Plan

## Goal

Secure 3 design partners in 60 days for paid or strongly committed pilots focused on oncology data harmonization, governance, and interoperability workflows.

## Design Partner Definition

A design partner should meet all criteria below:

- Owns oncology data pipelines across at least two systems (EHR, lab, registry, warehouse, trial platform, or external partner feeds)
- Feels recurring pain from inconsistent disease, gene, and variant terminology
- Has an operational owner who can evaluate workflow change in 2 to 6 weeks
- Can provide representative synthetic or de-identified sample records for evaluation
- Willing to share baseline effort metrics and pilot outcomes

## Ideal Customer Profile

Primary segments:

1. Academic cancer centers with molecular pathology and translational informatics teams
2. Molecular diagnostic laboratories handling high oncology test volume
3. CROs or trial data teams integrating multi-site oncology data

Secondary segments:

1. Pharma translational oncology and real-world evidence teams
2. Healthcare data platforms with oncology modules

## Buying Committee Map

For each account, identify at least one champion and one economic stakeholder.

Champion titles:

- Director of Molecular Pathology Informatics
- Oncology Data Engineering Lead
- Clinical Genomics Program Manager
- Bioinformatics Director

Economic stakeholder titles:

- VP of Precision Medicine
- Chief Medical Information Officer
- VP of Clinical Data Science
- Head of Real-World Evidence

## Outreach Narrative

Problem:

- Oncology programs lose analyst and curator time to repeated terminology cleanup before cohorting, reporting, and analytics.

Offer:

- 30-minute workflow fit call
- 2-week pilot using sample records
- Measured before/after on manual review load, turnaround time, and reconciliation confidence transparency

Differentiators to emphasize:

- Human-governed review workflow instead of black-box auto-mapping
- Evidence-backed reconciliation with provenance and audit history
- Standards-oriented export path for FHIR and OMOP workflows

## 60-Day Execution Plan

Week 1 to 2:

1. Build a list of 40 target accounts
2. Prioritize top 15 by urgency and data maturity
3. Send outreach to at least 3 contacts per target account

Week 3 to 4:

1. Complete 10 discovery calls
2. Run 5 product-fit demos
3. Convert 2 organizations to pilot scoping

Week 5 to 8:

1. Launch 2 to 3 pilots
2. Publish pilot scorecards with agreed metrics
3. Convert at least 1 pilot into paid continuation

## Qualification Scorecard

Score each account 1 to 5 across each dimension:

- Pain severity
- Data complexity
- Access to decision maker
- Pilot urgency
- Security and compliance readiness
- Budget ownership clarity

Prioritize accounts scoring at least 22 of 30.

## Pilot Success Metrics

Track baseline and pilot values for:

- Percent records routed to review-required versus auto-reconcile
- Manual adjudication turnaround time
- Reviewer agreement rate
- Terminology normalization coverage
- Export readiness for downstream analytics teams

## Required Assets Before Outreach

- One-page value proposition tailored by segment
- Pilot one-pager with scope, timeline, data requirements, and outcomes
- Security and data handling summary for synthetic and de-identified inputs
- 5-minute demo flow matching design-partner workflows

## Weekly Operating Cadence

- Monday: pipeline review and targeting updates
- Wednesday: outreach and follow-up sprint
- Friday: call debrief, qualification scoring, next actions

## Risks and Mitigations

Risk: interest without urgency

- Mitigation: require a quantified current-state pain estimate in first call

Risk: technical curiosity but no budget

- Mitigation: involve economic stakeholder by second meeting

Risk: long legal cycle for data access

- Mitigation: start with synthetic or pre-approved de-identified sample set
