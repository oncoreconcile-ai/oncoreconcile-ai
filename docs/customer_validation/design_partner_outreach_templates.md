## Discovery Call Agenda (25 minutes)

1. Current workflow and systems involved
2. Highest-friction reconciliation scenarios
3. Governance and review process today
4. Desired pilot outcomes and constraints
5. Decision process and timeline

## Qualification Questions

1. Which team currently owns disease, gene, and variant normalization?
2. How many records require manual review per week?
3. What downstream systems consume the cleaned output?
4. What compliance boundaries apply to pilot data?
5. Who signs off on pilot success and next-step budget?
