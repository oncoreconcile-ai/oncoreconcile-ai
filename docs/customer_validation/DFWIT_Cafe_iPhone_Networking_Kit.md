## 20-Second Intro

We are building OncoReconcile AI. It helps oncology teams clean and govern messy disease, gene, and variant data before analytics and AI use it. The platform provides evidence, confidence scoring, and human review workflows so uncertain records are not forced into bad mappings.

## 45-Second Version

OncoReconcile AI is a precision oncology data quality and governance platform. We normalize disease, gene, and variant terminology, retrieve supporting evidence, and route ambiguous cases into accountable human review. We also provide standards-oriented export pathways for downstream interoperability and analytics workflows. We are looking for design partners to run short pilots and measure reduction in manual reconciliation effort.
