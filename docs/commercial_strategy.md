# OncoReconcile AI

# Commercial Strategy

## Executive Summary

OncoReconcile AI is a precision oncology data quality, governance, and interoperability platform designed to improve the consistency, explainability, and usability of clinical and genomic data.

The platform addresses one of the most persistent challenges in healthcare AI and precision medicine:

**poor data quality caused by inconsistent biomedical terminology.**

By combining AI-assisted reconciliation, evidence retrieval, human governance workflows, and interoperability exports, OncoReconcile AI helps organizations create trustworthy, AI-ready oncology datasets.
